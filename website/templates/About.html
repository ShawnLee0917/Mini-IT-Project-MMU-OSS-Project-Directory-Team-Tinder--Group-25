About MMU OSSD

The Multimedia University Open Source Software Directory — where student projects live on.

Our Mission

MMU OSSD exists to solve a simple but recurring problem: great student projects are built every semester, and most of them disappear the moment their creators graduate or move on. This platform gives Multimedia University students a place to list, discover, and hand over open-source projects, so good work can keep growing instead of sitting untouched in an old repository.

What You Can Do Here

  • Search and filter projects by tech stack, status, and activity.
  • Request to join a project team or hand your project to a new maintainer.
  • Leave comments, suggestions, and feedback directly on a project page.
  • Earn badges for contributing, maintaining, and growing projects on the platform.

Built by Students, for Students

MMU OSSD is developed and maintained by Multimedia University students as an ongoing project in its own right. If you run into a bug, have an idea, or want to contribute, we'd love to hear from you.
